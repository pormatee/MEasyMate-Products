# HORAJARN Knowledge Verification V2.3

Status: development side-by-side with HORAJARN FREE V1.

Purpose:
- Separate deterministic calculation from verification of the policy/formula used.
- Cross-check knowledge claims before they are allowed to influence prediction confidence.
- Quarantine conflicting reference-calendar sources instead of silently choosing one.
- Keep Base 4 as a calculated fact until a predictive rule is supported by a traceable source.

Verification rules:
1. CROSS_CHECKED requires at least two supporting sources for the same scoped claim.
2. A deterministic result may be CALCULATED while the method/policy is still UNVERIFIED.
3. SOURCE_CONFIRMED_SECONDARY is structural/reference knowledge and is not enough by itself to create a predictive rule.
4. UNVERIFIED claims cannot be promoted by wording, interpretation, or AI.
5. Conflicting calendar sources are quarantined and cannot become a golden vector until independently resolved.

V2.3 verified/cross-checked scope:
- Taksa cycle order: 1,2,3,4,7,5,8,6.
- Taksa role names: บริวาร อายุ เดช ศรี มูละ อุตสาหะ มนตรี กาลกิณี/กาลี.
- Taksa role meanings at broad role level (paraphrased, not source text).
- Seven Numbers Four Bases: structural existence of base 1/day, base 2/month, base 3/year, base 4/sum.
- Thai lunar calculation reference vectors are checked independently from prediction knowledge.

Still UNVERIFIED in V2.3:
- Exact Maha Taksa 108-period duration formula currently used by V1/V2 adapter.
- 06:00 Thai-astrology day boundary policy.
- Exact 18:00 Wednesday-night cutoff.
- Predictive meaning of Base 4.

No production UI is switched by this milestone.
